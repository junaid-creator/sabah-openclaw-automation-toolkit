# Narrowing a delivered review's platform targets

Use when the owner explicitly excludes platforms from an existing delivered draft and the immediate publisher requires the review and queue targets to match exactly. This is review revision, not publication recovery or permission to publish. Do not infer a standing subset policy from a single request.

1. Inspect the installed helper's arguments and exact-review validator before promising that a subset approval will work. In the verified reference-workflow path, the photo helper accepted only a job ID, and `REVIEW_TARGET_OR_ASSET_MISMATCH` guarded exact target equality. An owner instruction to exclude TikTok did not create a supported subset option. Do not run the unchanged all-target helper, alter an old receipt's binding, or weaken equality checks.
2. Reconcile the job before revision: validate the genuine original review receipt, inspect the read-only approval/operation/platform/schedule ledgers and Sheets POST_LOG, and check relevant remote history. Local absence alone does not prove remote absence. The unapproved-draft branch requires DRAFT / WAITING_APPROVAL / MANUAL, matching Sheet content and targets, and no durable approval, schedule or attempt. Existing, successful or uncertain attempts require publication reconciliation instead; never reset them into this branch.
3. When revision implementation is authorized, inspect/reuse `tools/sabah_revise_review_targets.py` if installed. Require a nonempty strict subset with no duplicates or new platforms. Under the publisher's existing lock and the review-state lock, archive the complete original state/receipt and explicit owner instruction before mutation. Preserve the job ID, original image, URL/hash, all captions/hashtags and prior authorization history. Do not treat an earlier approval as approval of a subsequently delivered revised review.
4. Invalidate current review eligibility durably before the external Sheet update, so failure or an uncertain write leaves publication blocked. Change only platform flags on the existing queue row, then reread targets and compare the entire content row unchanged. Recheck absence of attempts before finalizing local state. Retain the archive reference and original receipt in review history; create a new pending preview from the frozen content for only the retained platforms. Do not create a replacement job or resize/regenerate media. Local locks cannot prevent concurrent external Sheet edits; readback mismatches remain blocked for reconciliation, not automatic rollback or replay.
5. Use the existing claim → actual messaging tool → raw-result recording path for the revised preview. Preserve CLAIMED/UNKNOWN and never fabricate or reuse the original delivery ID. Verify both the archived original receipt and the new receipt, unchanged image hash/copy, and the new target set. Run the unchanged production exact-review gate read-only against fresh Sheets and asset data; a custom validator or successful send alone does not prove publisher acceptance.
6. Test strict-subset rejection, malformed/empty/duplicate targets, invalid original delivery, preservation of original state/copy/hash and refusal when any approval/attempt/schedule exists. Retain provenance and reliability regressions. The observed repair passed those tests and the live exact-review gate after genuine revised delivery; no publication was exercised. Do not generalize that evidence into crash-safe automatic resumption or completed publishing.

Completion: archived original evidence, unchanged creative/content, read-back reduced targets on the same job, genuine new delivery receipt, and passing existing exact-review admission. If the workflow requires fresh approval after revision, ask once for that revised review and stop before submission. Report excluded platforms explicitly; only a later valid approval may enter the normal publisher. A diagnostic or revision request is never that approval.
